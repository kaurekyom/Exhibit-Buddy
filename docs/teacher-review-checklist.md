# Teacher review checklist

Please review at least the first three guides or figures before the app is shared widely. Mark each box, or write what is wrong.

**Guide or figure reviewed:** ______________________  **Class level:** ________

## Science
- [ ] Every scientific statement is correct for this class level
- [ ] Formulas, numbers and units are correct
- [ ] The "How it works" explanation is correct and not misleading
- [ ] Simplifications are stated (for example "not to scale")
- [ ] Viva questions have correct, fair answers
- [ ] Glossary words are correct

## Built-in material to confirm
- [ ] Pinhole camera facts (section 2 of `docs/verification-report.md`)
- [ ] Paper parachute facts
- [ ] Animal cell: all 8 parts
- [ ] Carbon atom: 6 protons, 6 neutrons, 6 electrons, 2 and 4 in shells
- [ ] Solar system: order, orbit times, planet facts

## Safety
- [ ] Every material is suitable for the age group
- [ ] Every step with a sharp tool, heat, glue gun or pin says "Ask an adult"
- [ ] Safety section is complete and sensible
- [ ] No step needs mains electricity, flame or chemicals

## Build and dimensions
- [ ] Printed templates at 100%: the scale bar measures exactly 50 mm
- [ ] Part sizes agree with the materials table and the steps
- [ ] The model can really be built from the written steps (build one if you can)
- [ ] The 3D or tray picture agrees with each step

## Figures (cells, atoms and similar)
- [ ] Each part's name, summary and detail are correct
- [ ] Parts are the ones the syllabus expects at this level
- [ ] Quiz questions are fair

## Accessibility
- [ ] Text is readable; works with keyboard; works on a phone
