Stage 4 of 4. Here is my final index.html (pasted below).

   - Safety review: every material and step checked for suitability for a
     14-year-old, with adult-supervision labels confirmed.
   - Accessibility check: fonts, contrast, keyboard use, reduced motion.
   - Assumptions made and open risks, clearly marked.
   - Anything you could not verify, listed plainly.
